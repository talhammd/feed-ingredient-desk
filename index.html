How the desk works

Signals

  • Phase out: a compliance or residue risk outweighs price; stop new orders.
  • Buy / lock: price or supply conditions favour fixing volume and price now for the stated period.
  • Accumulate: add cover in tranches up to the stated days.
  • Wait: a known event (harvest, crush start, policy decision) is likely to bring better terms.
  • Hand-to-mouth: prices are soft or slipping; buy only the current run.
  • Hold cover: no edge either way; keep normal stock.
  • Get quotes: no reliable price yet; the desk needs supplier quotes before calling it.

Each signal is a desk view with its reasons, sources and date. The daily refresh re-reads the market and rewrites views that no one on the team has locked. A view changed by a team member stays locked until they release it. The board also checks every view against the data: it flags a view when the price has moved more than 5% in 30 days against it, when an offer is dear for its nutrients, or when the price is more than 21 days old.

Nutrient value

Each ingredient is priced as the mix of maize and 48% soybean meal that supplies the same poultry AMEn and crude protein (Petersen's method). At maize Rs65 and soybean meal Rs135 a kg, canola meal is worth about Rs107 a kg, rice polish about Rs63 and 60% corn gluten about Rs181. The table covers poultry ingredients; wanda-only materials need a ruminant energy basis. Values from NRC (1994) are marked NRC; trade averages are marked EST and should be replaced with your laboratory or NIR results.

Import parity

Landed cost adds insurance, customs, additional and regulatory duty, sales tax, income tax at import, port and inland costs, and the finance cost of the days the cash is tied up. Duty rates for soybeans, soybean meal and oilseeds come from USDA GAIN report PK2026-0004 (April 2026); confirm every rate with your clearing agent before contracting. Sales tax and income tax collected at import are shown separately because a registered manufacturer can usually adjust them.

ERP names

Where an ERP name carries another material in brackets, such as Millet HP (Molasses), the desk prices the material in brackets: molasses, palm kernel cake, urea and rapeseed meal. Gatawa could not be identified and is held for confirmation.
